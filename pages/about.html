Where it started

Izabela has been cleaning homes in Worcester for nearly two decades, first under the name Anna's Cleaning. The business was built word by word, referral by referral — because when you do great work, people talk.

The name change

We recently rebranded to Polished — a name that felt right for where we're headed. Same crew, same values, same commitment to leaving your home actually clean. Just a little shinier on the outside too.

What makes us different

Honestly? We just care more than most. Izabela responds quickly, works around your schedule, and stands behind the quality of every clean. You'll notice the difference.
